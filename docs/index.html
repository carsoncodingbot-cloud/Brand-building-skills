The Three Numbers On Your Bill

Solar isn't magic — it's arithmetic against a bill built from three parts. Understand them and every quote in your inbox (including ours) gets easier to judge.

≈35¢

The average rate — and climbing

SCE's average residential rate rose roughly 13% in one step in late 2025, to about 35¢ per kWh, with more increases proposed through 2028. Conservation nibbles; the trajectory bites.

4–9pm

The window priced against you

Time-of-use plans bill early evening — dinner, laundry, and the AC recovering from a 105° afternoon — at the day's highest rates. Summer peaks on some plans run past 50¢/kWh.

$24.15

The charge you can't conserve away

The fixed monthly Base Services Charge applies no matter how little you use. It's the part of the bill your thermostat discipline will never touch.
