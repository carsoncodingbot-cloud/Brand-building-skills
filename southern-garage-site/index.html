In plain English

What the specs actually mean.

No jargon you have to nod along to. If a term isn't here, ask. We'll answer in a sentence.

What does "gauge" mean?

It's steel thickness, and a lower number is thicker. Our standard frame is 14-gauge (12-gauge is the upgrade) and standard panels are 29-gauge (26-gauge for commercial).

What is a clear-span building?

No posts inside. The roof is carried by the trusses across the full width, so you get the whole floor to park, work and store on.

Regular, A-frame or vertical roof?

Regular and A-frame are the standard styles. A vertical roof runs the panels top to bottom so water, leaves and debris shed faster — it's the premium option.

What's a turn-down footing?

The slab is poured thicker along its edge — 8"×8" — so the walls sit on a solid, monolithic edge instead of a thin slab.

How much snow or wind can it take?

Standard is a 35 PSF roof load, with trusses 5 ft on center (4 ft for heavier builds). Tell us your county and we'll confirm what your site requires.
