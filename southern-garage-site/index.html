Straight answers

Questions we get every day.

What does steel gauge mean?

Gauge is steel thickness, and a lower number is thicker steel. Our standard frame is 14-gauge (12-gauge is the upgrade) and standard panels are 29-gauge (26-gauge for commercial buildings).

What is a clear-span building?

No support posts inside. The roof trusses carry the load across the full width, so the whole floor is usable for parking, work and storage.

What is a turn-down footing?

The slab is poured thicker along its edge — 8″ × 8″ — as one piece with the slab, so the walls sit on a solid, monolithic edge instead of a thin slab.

Regular, A-frame or vertical roof — which is better?

Regular and A-frame are the standard styles. A vertical roof runs the panels from peak to eave so water, leaves and debris shed faster. It's the premium option.
