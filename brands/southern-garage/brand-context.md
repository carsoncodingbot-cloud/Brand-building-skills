# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Southern Garage
- **Category**: Auto repair & custom automotive (independent shop)
- **Description**: A straight-talking Southern auto shop that fixes your daily driver right the first time and builds the truck you've always wanted.
- **Stage**: Established locally, pre-marketing (reputation built on word of mouth)
- **Website**: southerngarage.com (domain owned — site build/refresh is Phase 1 of the marketing plan)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Vehicle owners within a 25-mile radius — working families, tradespeople, and commuters who depend on their truck or car daily and don't trust dealerships to treat them fairly.
- **Secondary Audience**: Truck and classic-car enthusiasts in the region with disposable income for custom builds, lift kits, restorations, and performance work.
- **Key Problem**: They've been burned before — surprise charges, upsold repairs they didn't need, condescending service writers, and dealership prices. They want someone who explains the problem in plain English, quotes it honestly, and stands behind the work.
- **Their Language**: "Fixed right the first time," "didn't try to sell me anything I didn't need," "showed me the old part," "treats you like family," "my truck," "fair price," "actually answers the phone."

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Dealership-quality diagnostics and warranty with hometown honesty — plus a custom shop under the same roof, so the mechanic who fixes your brakes is the same crew that builds show trucks. Skill you can see, from people you know.
- **Competitors**: Franchise dealership service departments (high price, low trust), national chains like Firestone/Pep Boys (transactional, rotating staff), other independent shops (trusted but often no warranty, no online presence, long waits).
- **Market Position**: Premium-quality work at fair independent prices — the trusted local expert, not the cheapest bay in town.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Honest, skilled, welcoming, proud, no-nonsense
- **Tone**: Casual and warm, plainspoken, confident without bragging. Southern hospitality, not Southern caricature.
- **Voice Admires**: Duluth Trading (working-class wit), Buc-ee's (regional pride done big), YETI (craftsmanship earns premium)

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Honesty first, work you'd sign your name to, treat every customer like a neighbor, respect people's time and money, take pride in the craft
- **Mission**: To be the shop folks around here trust with the vehicles their lives run on — and the one they call when it's time to build something special.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Grow monthly repair orders 30% and book a steady pipeline of 2–3 custom builds per quarter within 12 months.
- **Key Metrics**: Monthly repair orders, average repair order value, Google Business Profile calls/direction requests, Google review count and rating, custom-build inquiries, email/SMS list size, repeat-customer rate.
