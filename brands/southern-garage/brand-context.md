# Brand Context

> Source of truth: **"The Southern Garage Content Machine" — Playbook v1.0** (internal, confidential — kept out of this repo). This file summarizes it so every skill in this repo works from the real brand. Where this file and the Playbook disagree, the Playbook wins.

## Brand
- **Name**: Southern Garage Co. — American Steel Builders
- **Category**: Steel buildings — custom garages, workshops, and metal structures (slab, steel, insulation — delivered and installed)
- **Description**: One crew, one invoice: Southern Garage builds real steel buildings with the slab, the steel, and the install inside one whole number.
- **Stage**: Operating, with a mature brand & content system (Playbook v1.0) already in force
- **Website**: southerngarage.com
- **Phone**: (205) 941-6115 — Alabama; text-first is the primary CTA

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Property owners in the metro radius who want a garage, shop, or steel building on their land — buyers who trust "40s–50s, weathered, flannel-real" and text from the couch at 9pm.
- **Key Problem**: Big-ticket construction is full of vague quotes, hidden line items, stock photos passed off as real work, and pressure tactics. They want the whole number up front, proof the builder actually built what's in the photos, and room to think without being chased.
- **Their Language**: "What's the whole price?", "is that installed?", "what's it run a month?", "is that y'all's actual work?", "text us your city."

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Verifiable honesty at big-ticket scale. Every photo is a real Southern Garage build or it's labeled a concept (the REAL SOUTHERN GARAGE BUILD badge). Whole-number pricing with the monthly bridge. One crew, one invoice, in writing.
- **Competitors**: Regional steel-building dealers and broker networks (quote-chasing, stock renders), carport/metal-building national brands (ship-and-leave, install extra), local general contractors (slow quotes, change orders).
- **Market Position**: Premium-trust, mid-market price — wins on verifiability and offer clarity, not on being cheapest.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Honest, unhurried, exact, workmanlike, permission-giving
- **Tone** (Voice Doctrine — Miner register): Detached, downward inflections, statements not exclamations. Prices live inside plain sentences ("His number was $12,699 plus tax."). Every close carries an exit ramp ("No harm if it just sits in a drawer."). One human beat per caption, maximum.
- **Offer register (Hormozi)**: Whole-number-first, monthly bridge on every big ticket, mechanism over adjective ("one crew, one invoice" beats "high quality"), real scarcity only (calendar slots, concrete season — never countdown timers).

## Visual Identity (fixed — see Playbook §2–3)
- **Color**: Brand Orange #FA5F1E (the ONLY accent), Canvas Dark #100D0A, footer #0C0A08; text white/#E4DDD5/#C8C8CE by tier
- **Type**: Montserrat only — 900 mastheads/prices, 800 subheads/pills, 700 body
- **Logo**: white rounded-rectangle plate lockup, placed as a sealed unit, never rebuilt or recolored
- **Badge**: "REAL SOUTHERN GARAGE BUILD" — unedited photos of performed work only; AI-touched images run badge-free and labeled "concept only"

## Values & Mission (the Three Pillars)
- **The Honesty Law**: If a claim, photo, or price can't be verified in writing, it does not ship. No AI buildings presented as real, no fabricated customers, no location claims without the job record.
- **The Offer Doctrine**: Lead with the whole number, bridge with the monthly, name the mechanism.
- **The Voice Doctrine**: Pressure released is trust gained; trust is what dials the phone.
- **Mission (working)**: Be the steel builder whose word is checkable — the brand is the moat, the honesty is the brand, the numbers are the aim.

## Goals
- **The Four Numbers (scoreboard)**: Revenue, average ticket, leads per month, close rate — every creative decision multiplies against them.
- **Supporting metrics**: Per-creative CTR, cost per lead, cost per *qualified* lead (typed ZIP in radius), rep speed-to-first-touch (under 5 minutes converts ~8×), wish-list anchor usage ("quote the dream, trim from there") as the average-ticket lever.

## Existing Marketing System (already in force — do not duplicate)
