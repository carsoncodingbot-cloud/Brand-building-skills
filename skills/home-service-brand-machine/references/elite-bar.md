# The Elite Bar — from correct to category-killing

The other references guarantee the laws. This one guarantees the ceiling.
A build that merely follows the laws is a B+. These are the moves that make
the finished site read as the most expensive company in its metro — and the
gate that stops anything less from shipping.

## 1. Study-the-leaders protocol (before styling anything)

Spend the first design pass emulating the top of the industry, never the
middle. Reference classes to draw from (patterns, never branding):

- National lead-gen machines (the Angi/Networx funnel discipline: one
  question on screen, progress, instant-feel) — take the funnel physics,
  reject their junk-lead volume posture.
- Private-equity flagship home-service brands (the $50M+ metro operators):
  cinematic dark heroes, real crew photography at scale, financing modules,
  number-dense proof bars.
- Top D2C landing pages (the $100M offer pages): one idea per viewport,
  relentless CTA rhythm, objection-killing section order.

Fuse their strongest patterns WITH this skill's laws (capture-first, honesty,
real photos). When a leader pattern conflicts with a law — e.g. their sticky
"Call Now" bars — adapt it: our sticky mobile bar opens the FUNNEL, with the
phone as the smaller secondary inside the confirmation flow.

## 2. The cinematic layer (what makes pages feel alive)

- **Hero:** full-bleed dark gradient or scrimmed real photo, the iced/layered
  display headline, one accent-colored word maximum, funnel card floating
  with real elevation (long soft shadow, not a border). Mascot/character
  interacting with the card where the brand has one.
- **Scroll-reveal:** sections fade/rise 12–20px on first entry (IntersectionObserver,
  0.5–0.7s ease-out, ONCE — no re-triggering). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Micro-interactions:** buttons lift 1–2px with shadow growth on hover;
  photo cards slow-zoom 5% over 700ms; option cards in the funnel give an
  instant pressed state (<100ms feedback). Nothing bounces, nothing spins.
- **Animated counters** only for TRUE numbers, counting up on reveal.
- **Section rhythm:** alternate dark/light with intentional transitions
  (wave dividers or hard color blocks — pick ONE language per site).
- **Depth system:** exactly three elevation levels (flat background, card,
  floating card). More reads as noise.

## 3. The conversion stack (every high-intent page)

Order matters — this is the objection gauntlet:

1. Hero: fear-killer headline + funnel (above the fold, mobile included).
2. Trust bar: the 3 strongest TRUE claims.
3. Proof: real photos / real numbers / real reviews (only what exists).
4. Service/offer blocks with per-block micro-CTAs into the funnel.
5. The honest-math section (repair-vs-replace, cost-of-waiting) — smart
   buyers convert on math, not adjectives.
6. Risk-reversal band ("the number doesn't move · say no anytime").
7. FAQ (schema-backed) answering the real objections.
8. Final capture section + ONE phone band.
9. **Mobile sticky bar** (appears after scrolling past the hero funnel):
   one red button, funnel-opening, with a thin phone text-link beside it.
   Never covers funnel inputs; hides while the funnel is open.

Scroll-depth re-prompt: one gentle funnel re-invite mid-page (a compact
inline card, never a popup). Zero exit-intent popups — they read cheap and
violate the premium posture.

## 4. Performance & polish floor

- Lighthouse mobile ≥90 performance, ≥95 accessibility/SEO on the built
  output. Images sized and compressed (hero ≤350KB, cards ≤200KB), fonts
  ≤2 families with `display: swap`, zero layout shift on load (reserve
  image space), interaction-ready <2s on 4G.
- Every tap target ≥44px. Contrast AA everywhere (scrims exist for this).
- The details that whisper expensive: consistent 4/8px spacing grid,
  one border-radius language, true optical centering (measured, not
  eyeballed), no default-blue focus rings (styled focus states), favicon +
  og-image + 404 page branded.

## 5. THE 9+ GATE (run before any delivery — no exceptions)

Score the build 1–10 on each. Anything below a 9 average goes back to work,
and each line below 8 gets fixed first:

| # | Dimension | The test |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1-second clarity | Screenshot the hero at 390px; does WHO+WHAT+WHY-CARE land before reading body text? |
| 2 | Funnel friction | Count actions from land → submitted lead. Par: 4 taps + 4 fields + 1 submit. Zero dead-ends. |
| 3 | Capture discipline | No phone CTA before capture anywhere in a funnel path, sticky bars included. |
| 4 | Trust density | Every viewport-height contains ≥1 TRUE proof element (photo, claim, process, review). |
| 5 | Alive-ness | Scroll the page: reveals, hovers, pressed states all firing; nothing static-brochure. |
| 6 | Mobile luxury | True-390px pass: no wraps/clips/overflows, thumb-reachable CTAs, 16px inputs, sticky bar behaving. |
| 7 | Copy teeth | Zero generic adjectives carrying a section; every headline is fear/mechanism/math. |
| 8 | Honesty audit | Grep for numbers; every one defensible today. |
| 9 | Speed | Lighthouse mobile ≥90 or measured equivalent. |
| 10 | Distinctiveness | Cover the logo: could this page belong to any competitor? If yes, it fails — add the brand's signature moves (palette, character, voice lines) until it couldn't. |

Record the ten scores in the delivery summary. The founder sees the
scorecard, not just the page — that's what justifies premium retainers.

## 6. Merging inspiration without theft

Emulate structure, rhythm, and interaction patterns freely — they are not
ownable. Never copy copy, never lift brand colors/marks/photos, never
imitate a specific competitor's distinctive assets in the same metro. The
goal is to out-execute the category's best patterns with OUR laws layered
on top — real photos, honest claims, capture-first — which is precisely the
combination the industry's template mills cannot ship.
