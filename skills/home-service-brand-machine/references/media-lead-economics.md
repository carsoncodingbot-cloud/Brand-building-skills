# Media Buying & Lead Economics

The governing equation: **CPL = CPM ÷ (CTR × CVR × 1000).** Creative attacks
CTR. Offer + funnel attack CVR. Engagement quality attacks CPM. Every
optimization is one of these three attacks; anything else is theater.

## 1. Meta buying SOP (platform-agnostic laws)

| Layer | Law |
|---|---|
| Structure | ONE broad ad set per market (residents, 28+, minimal interests — the creatives ARE the targeting) · 3–5 hook-distinct ads inside · CBO. Naming: `MKT | Objective | Funnel` / `MKT - Concept - Angle`. |
| Forms | Higher-intent + OTP verification + the grading questions. NEVER "more volume" forms — they optimize the dashboard and destroy the lead product. |
| Platform "help" | ALL AI creative enhancements OFF — and they silently re-enable on every duplicate: recheck every time. Use "Duplicate original setup." Opportunity scores and blue Apply buttons: categorically ignored. |
| Learning | 72-hour no-touch law after any change. Judge nothing on days 1–2; the first ~10 conversions are the algorithm's tuition. Founder panic-edits reset the education. |
| Kill / Feed | Kill: ≥$90 spent + 0 leads + CTR <0.7%. Feed winners +20% per 72h. Never judge long-cycle (replacement/build) ads on week-1 CPL. |
| Freshness | New creative every 2–3 weeks; moment ads (weather/season) re-cut weekly so they are never factually wrong. |
| Scaling | +20% steps, never doublings. Expect CPL to rise with budget (auction depth) — cheap and big don't coexist. |

**Benchmarks (local service lead-gen):** CTR fail <0.7% / operator 1–1.5% /
elite 2%+. CPM $15–25 operator, $10–18 elite. Form completion 15–25%
operator, 30%+ elite. CPL (verified higher-intent) $40–70 operator, $20–40
elite; teens only in demand spikes with elite creative. Week-1 CPL runs
$70–100 and falls as the pixel learns — tell the founder before day 1.

**Speed-to-lead < 5 minutes** is the single highest-ROI behavior in this
entire system — contact odds collapse ~21× between minute 5 and minute 30.
Wire the webhook → instant text-back automation → human call, in that order.

## 2. Lead grading matrix (form answers → price)

| Grade | Signals | Price (HVAC reference) |
|---|---|---|
| A | Owns + dead/urgent + old system (replacement-probable) | $85–100 |
| B | Owns + urgent + repair-shaped | $55–65 |
| C | Owns + soft urgency | $35–45 |
| D | Planner | hold/batch |
| F | Renter/unqualified | $0 — never invoiced (protects the book's trust) |

Buyer's real math (use it in the pitch): $350–600 average repair ticket,
~1-in-3 lead→revenue, replacement conversions $3–5k gross — expected profit
per exclusive lead $150–300+. Anchor defense: "Angi sells that same
homeowner to you AND three competitors for $60 each. I'm giving you the
only copy."

## 3. The Spread Doctrine

CPL is vanity; **spread = sale price − cost per SOLD lead** is the business.
Junk $15 leads that sell for $20 with refund wars are a negative-spread
business. Verified $45–55 leads selling exclusive at $55–100 compound. Grade
honestly, never invoice an F, timestamp freshness, sell exclusivity.

## 4. The money model ladder (Hormozi stages)

1. **Attraction offer** — the Grand Slam fear-killer, near-zero marginal cost.
2. **Core offer** — repair→replacement upgrade paths sold on honest math
   (the ⅓ rule).
3. **Continuity** — membership club (tune-ups, priority, discounts):
   recurring cash + owned audience + review engine. Every vertical has one.
4. **Downstream** — the lead book itself, wholesaled exclusive and graded.

## 5. Scale laws ($0 → 8 figures, constraint by constraint)

| Stage | Revenue | Constraint | The move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proof | $0–10k/mo | Metrics + Model | One market, one funnel, one offer. Prove CPL, contact rate, close rate, ticket, spread. Founder answers every lead <5 min. |
| Repeatability | $10–50k/mo | Money | Feed winner creative; launch continuity; standing orders from lead buyers; review engine on. |
| Capacity | $50–150k/mo | Manpower | Hire against the phone first — speed-to-lead is the first thing founders drop. |
| Management | $150–400k/mo | Manager | Weekly councils run without the founder; dashboards from the benchmarks above. |
| Multiplication | $400k+/mo | Market | Clone the machine: new metro or new vertical. Swap the skin tables, keep every law. |

The 20-hour rule: if a stage feels stuck and you haven't spent 20 focused
hours on its named constraint, you haven't started.

## 6. The weekly operating system (one page)

- **Every lead:** <5-min callback → grade → matrix price → route/sell fresh
  with timestamp. No answer: instant text + 1-hour re-call.
- **Daily (2 min):** delivery green · leads answered · NOTHING edited.
- **Tue/Wed:** one organic post (tip / job story / education).
- **Friday (30 min):** ads council — kill/feed by data; GSC Performance;
  log buyer prices.
- **Bi-weekly:** one new ad into rotation (4 renders + JPG twins + QA
  gates). Moment ads re-cut weekly.
- **Monthly:** 2–3 articles with branded covers · citation/link push · P&L:
  spend vs lead revenue vs spread.

## 7. Competitor analysis discipline

Ad Library longevity proves nothing by itself (no spend/result data shown —
a 3-year-old ad may be a low-budget zombie); the lesson is "always be
running," not "copy this ad." Estimate competitor revenue by triangulating:
published figures (ZoomInfo et al., treat as modeled), review count ÷
10–15% ask-rate × blended ticket, fleet/employee counts × $250–350k/head,
and marketing-behavior signals (radio, PR wire, multi-brand launches).
State ranges and label every number as "ours" or "estimate."
