## The fear table (Grand Slam Offer, §1.2)
**#1 buyer fear (CA solar 2026):** being locked into a 25-year contract they
never really read — escalators on page 11, dealer fees inside the payment,
liens surfacing at resale, dead tax credits quoted as live.

**Grand Slam attraction offer:** *Every number in writing before you sign —
system price (cash AND financed), payment, escalator (none by default), rate
assumptions, warranty chain, title impact. And if solar doesn't pencil for
your house, we say so in writing and you keep the analysis.*

Costless to deliver, kills the #1 fear entirely, un-copyable by commission-
driven competitors (their incentive structure can't afford it).

## Funnel (60-Second Solar Reality Check)
Q1 bill ($<150 / 150–300 / 300–500 / 500+) → value.
Q2 goal (kill bill / backup / both / curious) → product + intent.
Q3 timing (ASAP / months / researching) → grade.
Q4 own (own / rent / manage) → qualify.
Segments: rent · small (honest maybe-not = trust gold) · priority · battery · planner.

## Voice
Glacier voice laws apply verbatim (established, short sentences, mechanisms
over adjectives, only real offers, 0–2 emoji, ☀️ preferred). Solar-specific
additions: name the utility by name; never say "free solar"; never quote the
dead credit; "in daylight" is the house metaphor — use sparingly, one moment
per asset.
